Built for SnapMagic

A working prototype for SnapMagic's finance operations. Bill customers at month-end, then see who owes money every two weeks and what to send each of them. Built by Jake Makking-Robinson.

How it works ↗
Email signatureSignature

Columns the contracts file needs

Export active contracts or deals from HubSpot, or keep them in a Sheet, as CSV with one row per contract. Use these column names, or download the template. If your file names a column differently, you match it after choosing the file.

Slash dates are read as US (MM/DD/YYYY) unless the file shows they are day first, and you can choose when matching columns. ISO dates (2026-10-16) and "Oct 16, 2026" also work.

Last month to this month

Every dollar of change, contract by contract, so the total can be signed off before anything goes out.

Invoices

One invoice per customer, a line for each contract. Issued on the last day of the month, due on the customer's terms. Open one to check the lines, edit the cover email or download the invoice as a PDF.

How the billing run works
Columns the export needs

Export invoices from HubSpot or QuickBooks as CSV, one row per invoice, including paid ones if you want the late-history modifier. Use these column names, or download the template. If your export names a column differently, you match it after choosing the file.

Slash dates are read as US (MM/DD/YYYY) unless the file shows they are day first, and you can choose when matching columns. ISO dates (2026-10-16) and "Oct 16, 2026" also work.

Aging by customer

Each bar is one customer's open balance, split by days past due at the run date.

Chaser drafts

One email per customer, covering every open invoice. The tier comes from the oldest overdue invoice; tone moves a step with payment history. Tap a customer to open the draft, edit it in place, then copy or open in Gmail.

Make it run itself

    
A Google Apps Script with the same aging, rubric and templates as this page (it's the same code). Every 14 days it reads the invoice export in a Sheet, saves one Gmail draft per customer for you to review (replacing last run's drafts you didn't send), and posts the digest to Slack. Nothing sends without you.

    
    
      
  1. Paste the HubSpot or QuickBooks invoice export into a Google Sheet, in a tab named Invoices, headers in row 1.
    2. 
      
  2. Open Extensions › Apps Script, replace the contents of Code.gs with the script below, and save.
    3. 
      
  3. In Project Settings › Script properties, add SENDER_NAME, SENDER_ROLE, FOUNDER_NAME, FOUNDER_ROLE and a Slack incoming webhook as SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL.
    4. 
      
  4. Run setup once and grant access to Sheets, Gmail and external requests. It adds a trigger for every other Monday at 8am.
    5. 
      
  5. Run run once to check: drafts land in Gmail, the digest posts to Slack, and an AR runs tab logs the totals.
